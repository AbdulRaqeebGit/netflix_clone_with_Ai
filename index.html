Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you press play.

What is Cineflix?
Cineflix is a Netflix-inspired streaming demo built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap. It brings together a curated film and TV catalogue, live OMDb search and local accounts — no real subscription required.
How much does Cineflix cost?
Nothing. This is a portfolio and learning project. The sign-up flow and profiles are powered entirely by your browser's local storage.
Where can I watch?
Anywhere you have a modern browser. Your account, profile and My List stay on this device because the demo does not use a backend.
How do I reset my password?
Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen, confirm the email address for your local account, then set a new password. Everything is handled on this device.
What can I watch?
Explore a hand-picked collection of popular films and series — from Dune and Oppenheimer to Stranger Things and Breaking Bad. Use the search bar to explore even more titles through OMDb.
Is Cineflix good for kids?
The catalogue is a general-audience demo, but you can create a separate profile and add only the titles you want to your own My List.
